Financial Operations & Reporting System

Designing a structured finance workflow that connects daily administrative input, validation, journal generation, period reporting, cash visibility, and management review.

01. The Operational Problem

Daily records existed, but input, validation, and reporting were not yet one connected workflow.

The finance process needed to support routine administrative input while also producing information that could be reviewed consistently at management level.

The challenge was to connect data entry, completeness checks, account mapping, journal logic, period reporting, and cash visibility without making the workbook difficult for a non-technical user to maintain.

02. Workflow Architecture

The workbook is organized around a controlled path from transaction input to management review.

01

Admin input

Capture transaction details in a standardized 22-field entry layer.

02

Validation

Check completeness, identifiers, amount logic, and mapped categories.

03

Account mapping

Translate transaction categories into consistent debit and credit accounts.

04

Journal generation

Allow only records that pass validation to create structured journal lines.

05

Period reporting

Aggregate ready records into monthly reporting views.

06

Cash visibility

Roll opening cash, inflows, outflows, and ending cash forward by period.

07

Management review

Keep unresolved items visible instead of silently forcing them into reporting.

04. Validation Logic

Validation status controls whether a record can flow into the journal.

The workbook uses four workflow statuses and a broader validation framework of fifteen conditions. Seven checks are applied directly within the input workflow.

  • Ready: the record is complete and can generate journal lines.
  • Not started: the record exists but has not been completed.
  • Needs completion: required information is still missing.
  • Needs review: the record requires manual review before posting.

A test snapshot showed five ready records producing ten balanced journal lines, while one record with a missing reference remained on hold.

05. Before vs After

The improvement is the connection between operational input and reviewable financial output.

Before

Daily finance records and reporting needs existed as separate operational activities, with more manual interpretation between input and management review.

After

A structured workflow connects input, validation, account mapping, journal logic, period reporting, cash roll-forward, and review status.

06. Outcome & Status

The architecture is complete; operational adoption is the next milestone.

The integrated workbook architecture and portfolio documentation are complete. The design demonstrates a maintainable route from routine finance administration to structured financial reporting.

Operational approval, user acceptance, final role attribution, and handoff remain outside the completed portfolio milestone and are not presented here as finished outcomes.

Confidentiality: Public visuals and demonstration records use synthetic data only. Student, parent, staff, vendor, banking, and real transaction information are excluded.
